Research tools we host here. They run in the browser — no accounts, no server,
nothing uploaded — so you can use them on your own brews without handing your
data to anyone.

## Brew Simulator

A pour-over brew simulator and test bench: a 1.5D column model of extraction
that answers in uncertainty bands rather than single numbers, with screens for
logging real brews and calibrating the model against them.

[Open the simulator](/brew-simulator/){: .btn .btn--primary}
[About the project]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}){: .btn .btn--inverse}

The app lives at [`/brew-simulator/`](/brew-simulator/) and is deployed straight
from [paddleandbrew/brew-simulator](https://github.com/paddleandbrew/brew-simulator),
so it is always the current build.

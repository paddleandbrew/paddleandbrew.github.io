## The roastery

[Paddle &amp; Brew](https://paddleandbrew.us) is a micro-roastery in Richmond,
Virginia. It started with a neighbor, Rumie — a true coffee connoisseur — who
tasted a batch of home-roasted beans and said, *"This is the coffee I want to
wake up for, every morning."* That became the job: roast every batch backed by
science, a whole lot of love and a dash of magic.

The roastery obsesses over three P's — **products, processes, people**: premium
beans from around the world, small batches roasted for the nuance in them, and
every bean ethically sourced and ethically traded, seed to cup.

- Shop and full story: [paddleandbrew.us](https://paddleandbrew.us)
- Instagram: [@paddleandbrew](https://www.instagram.com/paddleandbrew/)
- Richmond, Virginia — 8401 Mayland Dr, Ste S, Richmond, VA 23294

## This site

This is the research group inside Paddle &amp; Brew, working in the open. It is
the "backed by science" half of that sentence: how water moves through a bed of
grounds, what actually ends up in the cup, and how much of it we can honestly
claim to predict.

Two things we try to hold to:

**Models come with error bars.** A number without an uncertainty band is a guess
wearing a lab coat. The [Brew Simulator]({{ '/brew-simulator/' | relative_url }})
reports bands, and its calibration screens exist so those bands can be tightened
against real measurements instead of assumed.

**Code is part of the write-up.** Where there is a model, there is something
runnable to go with it — see [Projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}).
Notes and experiment write-ups live under [Notes]({{ '/notes/' | relative_url }}).

What we learn here feeds the roast profiles and brew guidance that leave the
roastery. Publishing it is deliberate: the rest of the trade is welcome to check
our work, and to tell us where it is wrong.

Paddle &amp; Brew is a small research and engineering effort around coffee: how
water moves through a bed of grounds, what actually comes out, and how much of
that we can honestly claim to predict.

Two things we try to hold to:

**Models come with error bars.** A number without an uncertainty band is a
guess wearing a lab coat. The simulator reports bands, and the calibration
screens exist so those bands can be tightened against real measurements
instead of assumed.

**Code is part of the write-up.** Where there is a model, there is something
runnable to go with it — see [Projects]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}).

Notes and experiment write-ups live under [Notes]({{ '/notes/' | relative_url }}).
The site itself is built with Jekyll and the
[Minimal Mistakes](https://mmistakes.github.io/minimal-mistakes/) theme.

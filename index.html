Paddle & Brew is a micro-roastery in Richmond, Virginia, where every batch is roasted with precision, love and a dash of magic. This site is where our research group works: the models we are still arguing with, the measurements that did not go as planned, and the tools we build to answer our own questions about coffee.

We do this because roasting decisions and brew recommendations should rest on something more than folklore. What we learn here goes back into the coffee we send out — and we publish it, because the rest of the trade is welcome to check our work.

Brew Simulator

A pour-over brew simulator and test bench — a 1.5D column model of extraction with honest uncertainty bands. It runs entirely in your browser on a WebAssembly solver; nothing is uploaded.
